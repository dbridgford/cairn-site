Cairn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Cairn (for Mac, iPhone and iPad) keeps your notes on your own devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud.

What we collect: nothing. Cairn has no account system, sends nothing to us or to any server we run, and contains
no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Where your data is stored
- On each device, in Cairn's library (on the Mac, ~/Cairn Library by default; on iPhone and iPad, inside the app).
- If you turn on iCloud Sync, a copy is stored in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), under your Apple
  Account. Only your Apple Account can read it; we cannot. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
- Locked notes are encrypted on your device with your password before they are stored or synced. The password is
  never stored or sent anywhere. On iPhone, if you choose Face ID unlock, the password is kept in the iPhone's
  Keychain and can only be read after Face ID.

On-device processing
- Text recognition (OCR) of scans and photos uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.
- Dictation on iPhone uses Apple's speech recognition, on the device where the device supports it.
- Ask Cairn on the Mac uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model where available, otherwise a built-in interpreter.

Permissions (iPhone)
- Camera: only when you scan a document. Microphone and Speech Recognition: only when you dictate.
- Photos: only the photos you pick. Notifications: reminders for tasks you give a due date.

Other
- When importing from Evernote, Cairn only reads Evernote's local files on your Mac and never changes them.
- Links in your notes open in your browser only when you tap or click them.
- Backups: the Mac app saves a daily copy of its database inside your library folder (Backups/).

Children: Cairn is a general productivity app and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the Cairn website.
